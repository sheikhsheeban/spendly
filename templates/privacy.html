Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, Spendly collects only the information you give us:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions of the expenses you record.
  • Basic usage information — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly, including to:

  • Display your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to run the Service; these providers process data only on our behalf.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the fonts are requested.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to, or deletion of, your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
